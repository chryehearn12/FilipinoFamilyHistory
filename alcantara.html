Alcantara, Cebu

Alcantara is a municipality on the western side of Cebu. In this family history website, Alcantara is included as a place connected to land records, family movement, cultivation, and local settlement history.

Research Notes

Alcantara appears in early twentieth-century notarial records connected to land possession and cultivation. Some records describe parcels of land by the amount of maize that could be planted there, and by the names of neighbouring landholders.

These records are useful because they show not only individual ancestors, but also the wider network of neighbours, land boundaries, occupations, and settlement patterns.
