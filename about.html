About This Site

This website is dedicated to Filipino genealogy research.

Filipino Family History is a personal genealogy indexing project dedicated to making historical parish records from the Philippines easier to search and access.

Many Filipino family records exist only as handwritten church registers, which can be difficult to read, search or navigate.This website aims to provide

simple, searchable indexes from Catholic parish registers, helping families locate records more efficiently.

This project currently focuses on records from Moalboal, Cebu, with indexed material including:

Baptism records

Confirmation records

Marriage records

Death records

Burial records

Most records date from the late 19th century to early 20th century, with coverage expanding gradually as more material is transcribed and verified.

Additional parishes and regions - including other parts of Cebu and Bohol - may be added in the future.

Note - records have been transcribed carefully from FamilySearch records.As with all historical records,transcription errors or inconsistencies may occur.

The search function is on the Home page, accessible via the sidebar.
